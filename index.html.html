The Studio & Curatorial Ethos

Every Painting Has a Story.

Founded as an intimate sanctuary for slow, contemplative art collecting, ARTÉ bridges the quiet discipline of the artist’s studio with modern architectural living spaces. We believe a room only truly comes alive when its walls hold something created by human hand, patience, and pigment.

Our studio works directly with independent contemporary painters across India—focusing on tactile brushwork, archival mineral pigments, raw linen canvases, and timeless tonal restraint. We reject mass reproduction: every piece in our catalogue is strictly a one-of-one original painting.

Whether it is the quiet mist of a coastal horizon or the raw impasto of an abstract study, each painting carries the memory of the hours it took to emerge.

Aarav Deshmukh & Studio Collective
Principal Curator & Resident Painter, ARTÉ
